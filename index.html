Publishing runs on official platform APIs, so some channels carry their own platform limits — the app always tells you before you hit one.

Private by design.

Designs, captions, schedules and channel tokens live on your device in secure storage — no analytics SDKs, no ads, nothing sold. An optional Sosial Cloud account (Singapore-hosted) keeps just your identity, team name and preferences so they survive reinstalls; a per-channel Cloud switch (off by default) holds an encrypted token copy for publishing while the app is closed. Disconnect a channel and its credentials are gone everywhere.
